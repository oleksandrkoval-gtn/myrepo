pulse sensors etc. may be not connected completely. This will lead to entries in the
error logs. In addition, all entries must be considered together within the same
period of time (timestamp). In many cases one error may create further other error
entries. e.g.: If a PCB is defective, this may entail another error message regarding
the missing CAN bus registration of the PCB in question. This additional error will
also disappear by replacement of the PCB.
